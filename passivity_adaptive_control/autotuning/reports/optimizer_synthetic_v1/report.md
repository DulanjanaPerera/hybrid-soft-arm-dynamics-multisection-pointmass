# Synthetic PAC optimizer demonstration

No hardware or real-arm optimization was performed. Bounds and objective are software-test examples.

30 observations, including explicit repeats, one known fault and one missing-data observation. Best observed feasible score: 0.001. Stop: observation_budget_exhausted.

GP uncertainty is model-dependent; feasibility score is a kernel-weighted frequency, not a safety guarantee. Fixed seed/configuration and raw observations reproduce proposals.

Recorded synthetic duration: 240 s. Unique measured locations: 24. Maximum repeats: 3. Replicate means/std/standard errors are in replicate_summary.csv; singleton std=0 is not proof of zero noise.
